Dissertation Research — AMCAF

Automated Multi-Cloud Compliance Assurance Framework

Final Confirmed Dissertation Mark: 68.4% — Merit

My postgraduate research focused on the complexities of maintaining consistent governance, risk management and regulatory compliance across heterogeneous cloud environments within regulated financial institutions.

Using Design Science Research methodology, I designed and developed the Automated Multi-Cloud Compliance Assurance Framework (AMCAF), a research-led prototype exploring how regulatory control objectives can be consistently mapped, assessed and validated across multiple cloud platforms.

The framework incorporates provider-specific data collection, automated control checks, cross-platform compliance mapping and structured reporting to support improved transparency, auditability and evidence-based compliance assurance.

Cloud Platforms

  • Microsoft Azure, including Microsoft Entra ID
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS)
  • Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

Research Focus

  • Multi-cloud governance and compliance assurance
  • Automated security and regulatory control validation
  • Identity and access management
  • Encryption and data protection
  • Security logging, monitoring and incident management
  • Regulatory control mapping
  • Evidence collection and auditability
  • Cloud operational resilience
  • Reducing governance complexity across heterogeneous cloud environments

Regulatory and Governance Frameworks

The research considered regulatory and governance requirements relevant to international financial services, including:

  • Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)
  • Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) regulatory expectations
  • UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
  • ISO/IEC 27001
  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework
  • Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) Technology Risk Management Guidelines
  • Luxembourg CSSF regulatory requirements
  • PCI DSS and SOC 2 control considerations

Research Contribution

AMCAF demonstrates how structured automation can support more consistent assessment of regulatory control objectives across cloud platforms, identify compliance gaps and produce evidence suitable for governance and audit review.

The research reinforces the importance of integrating cybersecurity, governance and operational resilience into enterprise cloud strategy rather than treating compliance as a separate or retrospective activity.

AMCAF is a research prototype. Its capabilities and compliance outputs are not independently certified or production-proven.
