Module 8: MSc Computing Project

AMCAF — Automated Multi-Cloud Compliance Assurance Framework
Grade: Merit

About the Project

My capstone project, submitted in January 2026, addresses a practical problem facing regulated financial institutions: demonstrating consistent compliance across multi-cloud environments where AWS, Azure, and GCP implement equivalent security and governance controls in technically different ways.

The Automated Multi-Cloud Compliance Assurance Framework (AMCAF) is a provider-agnostic, rule-based validation engine developed using Design Science Research methodology. It translates regulatory obligations — including DORA, FCA PS21/3, UK GDPR, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, CSSF 22/806, NDPR, MAS TRM, PCI DSS v4.0, and SOC 2 — into 20 technology-neutral control objectives, maps them to their equivalent technical implementations across cloud providers, and validates live configurations against those controls to produce structured, auditable compliance evidence.

The dissertation and accompanying presentation below set out the research motivation, the framework's four-layer architecture, and the evaluation of its reliability and practical utility in a regulated financial services context.
